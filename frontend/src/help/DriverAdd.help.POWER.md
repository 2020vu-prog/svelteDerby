# Driver Data Transfer

Download or upload the event's driver data when performing bulk maintenance. Review imports carefully because matching driver records may be updated.

When editing the CSV in Excel or Sheets, save/export it as **CSV UTF-8**, not the plain **CSV** option. A plain CSV export uses your system's legacy encoding, which turns curly quotes, accented letters, and em dashes into a "�" character on upload — and that corruption can't be undone once imported, so the field will need to be retyped by hand.
